Welcome to Our Brownsville
Dental Practice



Welcome to our Brownsville dental practice! We take great pride in providing our patients with the highest standard of care. Our conservative approach, combined with a friendly and knowledgeable staff, ensures that you’ll leave feeling confident about your dental health.

Dr. Jeffrey Carl has been caring for the Brownsville community for over 25 years, and with the recent addition of Dr. Austin Brunson, we’re excited to continue growing. Dr. Brunson’s expanding knowledge and skills are a wonderful complement to our team.

In addition to general dental care, we offer a wide range of services, including Invisalign, cosmetic dentistry, implant restorations, and more, to meet all your dental needs under one roof.

Meet Dr. Jeffrey Carl


Dr. Carl has roots to the mid-valley area with family grass seed farming in Tangent. He finished high school in Corvallis and completed an undergraduate degree at Oregon State University. Following that, Dr. Carl went to Oregon Health Sciences University School of Dentistry and graduated in 1996. He returned to Albany that same year and joined his childhood dentist to begin private practice.

Meet Dr. Austin Brunson


Dr. Brunson was born and raised in Albany, Oregon. He did his undergraduate studies at Oregon State University, and then attended Dental School at Roseman University of Health Sciences in South Jordan, Utah where he received a doctorate in dental medicine. He returned to Albany to practice dentistry with his childhood dentist, Dr. Jeffrey Carl.
