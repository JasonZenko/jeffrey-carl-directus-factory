Gregory Van Gordon, DMD
Dr. Van Gordon was born and raised in Portland, Oregon. He earned his Bachelor of Science degree from the University of Oregon and received his DMD degree from Oregon Health and Sciences University, graduating with honors. While at OHSU, Dr. Van Gordon was inducted as a member of the Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society. Following graduation, Dr. Van Gordon completed two years of specialty training and received his specialty Certificate in Endodontics from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston, Massachusetts.
Dr. Van Gordon is dedicated to the highest level of specialized care, technology and latest techniques to ensure that treatment is as comfortable and successful as possible. In addition to dentistry, Dr. Van Gordon has been involved with service projects with NW Medical Teams and Mission of Mercy in Portland, Oregon.
