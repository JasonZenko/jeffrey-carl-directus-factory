Welcome to Lowen Perio


Dr. Krista Lowen & Dr. Lillian Nguyen and their professional staff work closely with general dentists in the greater Portland area to provide patients with the very best in periodontal care and implant dentistry. Regardless of the extent of your periodontal needs, our team looks forward to establishing a solid relationship with you and providing you with the highest quality care.

We are currently accepting new patients and patient referrals. We thank you for your trust and interest. Please browse our website and learn how periodontal treatment can help you create a healthy, beautiful smile. For doctor referrals, please fill out the Doctor Referral Form. If you do not have a referral, please call the front desk to schedule your initial consultation appointment.

Meet the Doctors



Dr. Krista Lowen


Dr. Lowen grew up locally in Damascus, Oregon. She graduated from Northwestern University with a degree in Biomedical Engineering. She went on to attend dental school at the University of Texas Health Science Center at San Antonio where she graduated with honors. She completed her residency training in periodontics with a master’s degree from the University of Washington. During her residency she focused on advanced periodontal surgical techniques, implant dentistry, microsurgery and sedation dentistry.
More About Dr. Krista Lowen


Dr. Lillian Nguyen


Growing up in the suburbs of Portland, Dr. Lillian Nguyen attended Tigard High School and received her Bachelor of Science in Biochemistry from the University of Portland. She continued her studies at Oregon Health and Science University receiving her Doctorate in Dental Medicine and a Certificate in Periodontics and Master of Science. During her residency, Dr. Nguyen mastered the placement of dental implants, gum and bone grafting, the treatment of gum disease, and is certified in moderate sedation. She is board certified in Periodontology and Dental Implant Surgery.
