# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Patients and families in Albany and nearby Oregon communities who are comparing a local dentist, understanding a treatment, checking the doctors and practice, or finding a clear route to contact the office. Many visitors will be on a phone and may have accessibility, vision, dexterity, or dental-anxiety needs.

## Product Purpose

Present Jeffrey Carl DMD as an established, approachable local practice while preserving every factual claim and source relationship from the frozen website. Success means a visitor can understand the practice, reach the right treatment information, verify the doctors and contact the office without friction. For this noindex proof, success also means every rendered content node can be traced to the frozen source and edited through Directus without bypassing the fidelity gate.

## Brand Personality

Calm, neighbourly, established. The experience should feel like a well-run local clinical practice rather than a healthcare startup or a generic dental theme.

## Anti-references

- Sterile hospital portals with cold blue gradients and stock-smile photography.
- Generic AI landing pages built from repeating icon cards, oversized rounded panels and vague claims.
- Luxury-clinic minimalism that hides practical information behind sparse typography.
- The legacy site's cramped navigation, small body copy and fragile desktop-first layout.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve the evidence. Presentation can improve, but source copy, links, media, headings, embeds and metadata remain locked.
2. Make the practice tangible. Use the real doctors, office and treatment imagery already present in the capture.
3. Put practical actions within reach. Phone, address, service navigation and appointment routes remain visible without overwhelming the content.
4. Let long-form treatment information breathe. Clear hierarchy and readable measure beat decorative card grids.
5. Build one dependable system. Six page families share tokens and governed blocks while retaining family-specific rhythm.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain 4.5:1 body-text contrast, keyboard-visible focus, semantic landmarks, responsive layouts without horizontal overflow, reduced-motion support and minimum 44px interactive targets. Do not depend on color alone. Forms remain inert in the noindex proof and are labelled as review-only.
